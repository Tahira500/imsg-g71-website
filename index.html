Information for Parents

Access school timings, rules, syllabus, uniform regulations, and get directly in touch with our administration.

School Hours & Schedule

  • Monday – Thursday: 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM
  • Friday: 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM
  • Saturday & Sunday: Closed (Weekly Holiday)
  • Morning Assembly: Begins at 7:55 AM (punctuality is mandatory)
  • Recess: 11:00 AM – 11:30 AM

Uniform Policy (6th to 10th)

  • Summer: Sky blue kameez with white collar, white shalwar, and white dupatta/headscarf. Black school shoes and white socks.
  • Winter: Navy blue blazer/sweater, along with the summer uniform.

School Transport & Bus Details

  • Morning Pick-up: 7:00 AM – 7:45 AM
  • Afternoon Drop-off: 2:15 PM – 3:00 PM
  • Monthly Bus Fee: PKR 2,500
  • Active Routes: G-6, G-7, G-8, G-9, F-6, F-7, Islamabad Highway
  • Status: Active

School System Structure

  • Middle Section (Grades VI-VIII): Classes VI, VII, and VIII have **4 sections each (A, B, C, and D)**.
  • Secondary Section (Grades IX-X): Classes IX and X have **4 sections each (A, B, C, and D)**.
  • Matric Tech Program: A vocational track is active in Grade 9. **Section D of Class 9** is dedicated to Matric Tech.
  • Vocational Specialty: Matric Tech features hands-on cosmetology training in our dedicated Cosmetology Lab.

School Rules

  • Attendance: Minimum 75% attendance is required to qualify for Board examinations.
  • Punctuality: Late arrivals after 8:00 AM will not be allowed inside the school.
